AlphaTech AG Press Release Q1 2026

AlphaTech AG announced a strategic partnership with NordGrid Robotics to integrate predictive maintenance models into smart factory platforms.
The release states that pilot customers achieved lower machine downtime and faster fault detection during the initial rollout.
Management described the partnership as a catalyst for cross-selling opportunities in Germany and the Nordics.
At the same time, the company warned that integration expenses and recruiting costs for AI specialists will remain high in the next two quarters.
The board reaffirmed full-year guidance and emphasized confidence in the long-term outlook.
