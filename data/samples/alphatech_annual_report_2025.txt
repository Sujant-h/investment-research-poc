AlphaTech AG Annual Report 2025

AlphaTech AG reported revenue growth of 18 percent in fiscal year 2025 and improved its operating margin from 11 percent to 15 percent.
Management attributed the positive development to strong demand for industrial AI software and recurring maintenance contracts.
The company expanded into three new European markets and signed long-term contracts with automotive and logistics customers.
Free cash flow remained positive despite elevated capital expenditures linked to two product launches.
Net debt increased moderately after the acquisition of a robotics analytics specialist, but leverage remains within covenant limits.
Management expects continued growth in the industrial automation segment and highlighted a strong pipeline for 2026.
